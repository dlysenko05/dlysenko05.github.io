Featured research · Ioffe Institute

How absorbing gas partly covers a quasar

Working with Dr. Sergei Balashev, I analyze high-resolution spectra of quasar 4C 04.81. Fe II absorption lines reveal differences in effective covering across excitation levels. Whether they independently reveal the accretion disk's temperature structure is a more difficult question.
