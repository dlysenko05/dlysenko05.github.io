Computational study Magnetohydrodynamics

Torsional resistive MHD in a spherical shell

A focused reduced-model study of torsional resistive magnetohydrodynamics in a spherical shell, examining magnetic winding, Maxwell feedback, and Ohmic damping through spatially resolved PDE evolution. The project is intentionally presented as a reduced torsional study rather than as a full vector-MHD simulation.
