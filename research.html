Dielectric metasurfaces

ITMO University · Dr. Alexander Solomonov

I designed a 5 GHz all-dielectric Huygens meta-atom in COMSOL. A sweep of 51 radii gave 43 high-transmission states (T ≥ 0.9) spanning nearly a full 2π phase range.

I used that phase library to model a 377-element beam deflector and metalens. The deflector reached 20.49° in Lumerical FDTD and 19.71° in LightPipes for a 20° target. The two approaches gave different axial intensity maxima for the metalens, consistent with limits of the local-phase approximation at high numerical aperture.
