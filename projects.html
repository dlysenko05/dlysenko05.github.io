01 / Numerical PDEsNeutron-star magnetohydrodynamics

Torsional resistive MHD in a spherical shell

A reduced numerical study of magnetic winding, Maxwell stress feedback, and resistive damping in a rotating spherical shell.

The main evolution solver evolves a spatially resolved axisymmetric torsional PDE model. I investigated the competition between differential-rotation winding, Lorentz-force braking, and Ohmic dissipation. Related kinematic Dedalus calculations examined magnetic-field evolution in spherical geometries.

The torsional production model is deliberately reduced: it is not a complete three-dimensional, incompressible vector-MHD simulation in Dedalus.

Methods

PDE evolution, spectral and spherical-geometry calculations

Physical effects

Winding · Maxwell feedback · Ohmic diffusion

Scope

Reduced torsional model, not a full vector-MHD production solve

02 / Spectroscopic modelingQuasar 4C 04.81

Fe II partial-covering models

Fitting absorption profiles while testing which properties of the gas and source geometry are actually identifiable.

I extended SPECTRO analyses with homogeneous and inhomogeneous covering prescriptions, compared common and component-specific absorber topologies using AIC/BIC, and ran line-quality, profile-likelihood, and line-spread-function checks.

Excitation-dependent effective covering is supported in the present Fe II sample. A separate chromatic covering trend remains ambiguous, and absorption alone does not determine the disk temperature profile without assumptions about the spatial arrangement of absorbing gas.

Data

VLT/UVES spectra · Fe II / Fe II* transitions

Tools

Python · SPECTRO · profile likelihoods · model comparisons

Open issue

Disk–absorber geometry and wavelength dependence

03 / Bayesian computationGamma-ray bursts

GRB light-curve reconstruction

A Poisson-count inference pipeline for reconstructing gamma-ray burst temporal structure using random Fourier features.

The workflow uses Stan and CmdStanPy to fit count data with background estimates, posterior predictive checks, and model/sampler diagnostics. After synthetic pulse tests, I adapted it to Fermi/GBM time-tagged events and compared fits for GRB 200826A across detectors and temporal resolutions.

Broad pulse timing is reproducible in the tested data. Background tails, differences between detectors, and fine-scale pulse morphology are still being calibrated.

Likelihood

Exposure-aware Poisson detector counts

Model

Multiscale random Fourier features

Tools

Python · Stan/CmdStanPy · GDT · FITS

04 / Computational electromagneticsITMO University

5 GHz dielectric metasurface

Designing a high-transmission meta-atom library and building finite-aperture models for beam steering and focusing.

A COMSOL sweep yielded 43 radius choices with transmission ≥ 0.9. I generated a 377-element radial aperture, then compared Lumerical FDTD far-field and LightPipes scalar propagation estimates.

The 20° beam deflector agreed closely with its target (20.49° in FDTD; 19.71° in LightPipes). The 100 mm metalens target is not equally settled: different propagation models produced significantly different axial maxima.

Structure

Three-layer all-dielectric unit cell · 5 GHz

Computing

COMSOL · Lumerical FDTD · LightPipes · Python

Status

Beam steering validated in simulations; focusing comparison unresolved
