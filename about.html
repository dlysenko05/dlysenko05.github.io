About me

Academic background

I started studying physics at Novosibirsk State University in 2023 and transferred to Alferov University in 2024. I’m on the astrophysics track and expect to finish my B.Sc. in June 2027.

I joined the Ioffe Institute’s Theoretical Astrophysics Department as a research assistant in October 2024. I began there with numerical work on neutron-star structure. My current projects use spectroscopy to study gas around quasars and statistical models to reconstruct gamma-ray burst light curves.

Teaching

I tutor mathematics and physics in English and Russian through ProMath Online and independently. I work with GCSE and IGCSE, A-Level and IB students, and help students prepare for exams and competitions.

I also serve as a student group representative in Alferov University’s Department of Physics.
