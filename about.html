About me

Physics and astrophysics

I’m a physics undergraduate at Alferov University and a research assistant at the Ioffe Institute in St. Petersburg.

I began my research with numerical work on neutron-star structure. I now use absorption spectroscopy to study gas around quasars and statistical models to reconstruct gamma-ray burst light curves.
